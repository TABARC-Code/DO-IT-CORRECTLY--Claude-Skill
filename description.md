# do-it-correctly: description

## Ok Simply Put
Reads the small print so you don't have to, then tells you what it does to the thing you're building.
---
So yes it goes further than "here are their terms". It traces which clauses travel downstream into your finished app, track or book (attribution, share-alike, no-caching, no-AI-training).

When a terms page is locked or missing, it works from indirect signals and labels everything as inferred, never confirmed. It'll treat the fetched page as untrusted text, so a terms page that tries to instruct an AI gets flagged and ignored. Repeat checks on the same source collapse to one line.

Advisory only. Not legal advice. If it can't confirm something, it says so, which is the point.
